# BBBox Link — Legal & Support

Public-facing legal and support pages for the **BBBox Link** iOS app, hosted as
a static site (GitHub Pages) so the URLs can be referenced in the App Store
listing and in the app’s About screen.

## Live URLs

| Page | URL | App Store Connect field |
|------|-----|-------------------------|
| Privacy Policy | https://aeoberto.github.io/bbbox-link-legal/index.html | Privacy Policy URL |
| Support | https://aeoberto.github.io/bbbox-link-legal/support.html | Support URL |

These URLs are also defined in the app as `AppExternalURL.privacyPolicy` and
`AppExternalURL.support` (`BB-SSB_SW/BBBoxLink`).

## About BBBox Link

BBBox Link is the official BarBoards Box technician tool for manufacturing,
QA/QC, troubleshooting, and field service. It connects to BarBoards Box
hardware over Bluetooth Low Energy.

- **Display name:** BBBox Link
- **Bundle ID:** `tv.barboards.bboxlink`
- **Min iOS:** 16.0
- **Engineered by:** IO-TEQ
- **Brand:** BarBoards

The app does not collect personal data for server transmission, does not
require an account, and does not use third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
See `index.html` for the full privacy policy.
