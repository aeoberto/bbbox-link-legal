Privacy Policy – BBBox Link

Effective date: June 22, 2026

App: BBBox Link (tv.barboards.bboxlink)
Developer: IO-TEQ (engineering contractor for BarBoards)

BBBox Link is the official technician tool for the BarBoards Box (BB-SSB) hardware platform. It is used for manufacturing, QA/QC, troubleshooting, and field service. This policy describes what the app accesses on your device, how that information is used, and the choices available to you.

Summary: BBBox Link does not collect personal data for transmission to BarBoards, IO-TEQ, or any third party. The app does not require an account, does not use analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or tracking SDKs, and does not send information to our servers. Communication with a BarBoards Box happens locally over Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE). Data leaves your device only if you choose to share it (for example, by exporting a support report through the iOS share sheet).

Information the App Accesses

Bluetooth (required)

BBBox Link uses your device’s Bluetooth radio to discover and connect to nearby BarBoards Box hardware. While connected, the app may exchange:

  • Device identifiers (for example BB_<uid>)
  • Supervisor, SoM, switcher, fan, and power status telemetry
  • Configuration and diagnostic commands you initiate

This data is exchanged between your iOS device and the connected BarBoards Box. The app does not upload it to BarBoards, IO-TEQ, or any third-party server.

The app may use the bluetooth-central background mode so an active link can be restored after the app is backgrounded or relaunched. Bluetooth is not used when you are not using the app for device connectivity.

On-device storage

BBBox Link stores the following locally on your iOS device:

  • App preferences (iOS UserDefaults) – UI settings (for example splash-on-launch), onboarding completion, optional auto-reconnect preference, last connected device identifier, BLE restoration context, and 3D box-model viewer navigation settings.
  • Mobile operator tokens (iOS Keychain) – Per-device credentials you enter to unlock operator access on a BarBoards Box. One token may be stored per box UID. These tokens are provisioned to the box firmware and are separate from Jetson or venue credentials. They remain on your device until you clear them or delete the app.
  • Temporary support export files – When you export a diagnostics bundle, a JSON file and plain-text summary are written to the app’s temporary directory for the iOS share sheet. They are not transmitted unless you choose an action (Mail, AirDrop, etc.). In the app, use Overview Export support report.

Operator session vs. stored token

After you unlock a box with a mobile operator token, the app establishes an operator BLE session with the connected BarBoards Box. That session is bound to the connection and may expire according to firmware policy (for example, after a period of inactivity). The mobile operator token stored in Keychain is the credential you reuse to unlock again; it is not uploaded off-device by the app.

What we do not access

  • Camera or microphone (the 3D box viewer uses non-AR rendering only)
  • Photos, contacts, or location
  • Local network, Wi‑Fi scanning, or internet connectivity for app features

Tracking and Third-Party Services

BBBox Link does not track you across apps or websites. The app’s privacy manifest declares NSPrivacyTracking as false and lists no collected data types for App Store privacy labeling.

The app does not integrate third-party analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or social SDKs. It communicates only with BarBoards Box hardware over BLE.

Your Choices

  • Bluetooth permission – Revoke access at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Bluetooth → BBBox Link. Without Bluetooth, the app cannot discover or connect to a box.
  • Clear saved operator tokens – In the app, open App Settings → Security → Clear all saved operator tokens.
  • Remove all local app data – Delete BBBox Link from your device. This removes preferences, Keychain tokens, and temporary export files associated with the app.
  • Support export – Sharing a diagnostics bundle is optional and under your control via the iOS share sheet.

Data Retention

Because the app does not transmit data to IO-TEQ or BarBoards servers, there is no server-side retention of your app activity. Locally stored preferences and Keychain tokens remain on your device until you clear them, use in-app clear actions, or delete the app.

Security

Communication with a BarBoards Box uses Bluetooth Low Energy. Operator-level commands require unlock with a mobile operator token provisioned on the box and stored in the iOS Keychain on your device. Keychain entries use device-only accessibility after first unlock.

Children’s Privacy

BBBox Link is a professional technician tool and is not directed to children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
