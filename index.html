Privacy Policy – BBBox Link

Effective Date: 2026

BBBox Link is a technician tool for the BarBoards Box (BB-SSB) hardware platform. It is used for manufacturing, QA/QC, troubleshooting, and field service by authorized BarBoards and IO-TEQ personnel. This policy describes what data the app accesses, how it is used, and your choices.

Summary: BBBox Link does not collect personal data, does not use analytics or tracking, does not require an account, and does not send any information to our servers or any third party. All communication happens locally over Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) between the iOS device and a nearby BarBoards Box.

Information We Access

Bluetooth (Required)

BBBox Link uses your device's Bluetooth radio to discover and connect to BarBoards Box hardware. The app exchanges device identifiers (e.g. BB_<uid>), status telemetry, configuration, and diagnostic commands with the connected BarBoards Box. This data stays between your iOS device and the BarBoards Box. None of it is transmitted to BarBoards, IO-TEQ, or any third-party server by the app.

On-Device Storage

BBBox Link stores the following locally on your iOS device:

  • App preferences – Last connected device UID, UI settings, and similar non-sensitive state.
  • Operator session token – A short-lived BLE session token used to authenticate operator commands with a paired BarBoards Box. This token is stored in the iOS Keychain on your device and is not transmitted off-device.

What We Do Not Do

  • We do not collect, transmit, or store your personal data on any server.
  • We do not require an email, phone number, account, or login.
  • We do not use third-party analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or location.
  • We do not sell, share, or monetize any data.

Third-Party Services

BBBox Link does not integrate with any third-party services. The app communicates only with BarBoards Box hardware over BLE.

Your Choices

  • Bluetooth permission – You can revoke Bluetooth permission at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Bluetooth → BBBox Link. Without Bluetooth, the app cannot connect to a BarBoards Box.
  • Clear local data – Deleting the app from your device removes all locally stored preferences and Keychain tokens associated with BBBox Link.

Data Retention

Because BBBox Link does not transmit data off your device, there is no server-side retention. Locally stored preferences and Keychain tokens remain on your device until you clear them or delete the app.

Security

Communication between the app and the BarBoards Box uses Bluetooth Low Energy. Operator-level commands require an authenticated BLE session bound to a token provisioned to the BarBoards Box and stored in the iOS Keychain on your device.

Children's Privacy

BBBox Link is a professional technician tool and is not directed to children. The app does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.
